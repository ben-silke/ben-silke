The view

The panorama looks west-south-west from Mount Ainslie, over the city to the Tidbinbilla Range and the Brindabellas behind it. Every peak sits at its true compass bearing, with Telstra Tower on Black Mountain in the foreground. Heights are stretched about thirteen times so the folds read, and the sun's height follows your clock.
